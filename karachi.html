Coastal Hub & Economy

Karachi is the largest city in Pakistan and the capital of Sindh province. Located on the Arabian Sea coast, it serves as the country's main financial hub, featuring bustling markets, beautiful beaches like Clifton, and the historic Quaid-e-Azam Mausoleum.
