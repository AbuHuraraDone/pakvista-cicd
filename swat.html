Lush Valleys & Rivers

Swat is a valley and an administrative district in the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa province. Known for its high mountains, green meadows, and clear lakes, Swat is a prime destination for hikers and nature lovers looking to explore Kalam and Malam Jabba.
