Alpine Landscapes & Heritage

Hunza is a breathtaking mountainous valley located in the northernmost part of Gilgit-Baltistan. Surrounded by majestic peaks like Rakaposhi and Ladyfinger, it offers stunning views, historic forts like Baltit and Altit, and the serene Attabad Lake.
